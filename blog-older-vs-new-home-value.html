Which Has More Value: An Older Home or a New Home?

A comprehensive look at the advantages of newly built apartments versus older homes in the Siliguri and West Bengal real estate market.

When searching for the perfect property in Siliguri or anywhere in West Bengal, a common question arises: Which has more value, an older home or a new home?

This is really just a matter of preference, but both newer and older homes offer distinct advantages, depending upon your unique taste and lifestyle in the dynamic Siliguri real estate market.

The Charm and Landscaping of Older Homes

Older homes can generally cost less than new homes, however, there are many cases where new homes can also cost less than older homes. Most new homes will not have any backyard landscaping and some don’t include any front landscaping either. With an older home, the landscaping is normally already completed and could have tens of thousands of rupees in landscaping done, which is included in the purchase price.

Taxes on some older homes may also be lower. Some people are charmed by the elegance of an older home but shy away because they’re concerned about potential maintenance costs. Consider a home warranty to get the peace of mind you deserve. A good Home Warranty plan protects you against unexpected repairs on many home systems and appliances for a full year or more after you move in. Older homes can be better judged for their quality and timeless beauty.

The Modern Advantages of New Homes

In a new house, you can pick your own colour schemes, flooring, kitchen cabinets, appliances, custom wiring for TVs, electrical, computers, phones, and speakers, as well as have more upgrade options. Modern features like media rooms, extra-large closets, and extra-large bathrooms are also more attainable in ground-up construction.

In a used home, you rely largely on the previous resident’s tastes and technological whims, unless you plan to farm thousands into remodelling and rewiring. New-home designers can use new building materials such as glazed Energy Star windows, thicker insulation, and other technology that will lower future energy costs for the owner. Most regions now have minimum energy-efficiency requirements for new construction.

Safety, Energy Efficiency, and Construction Standards

Kitchens and laundry areas in new homes are designed to house more efficient energy-saving appliances. Older homes, unless they have undergone an energy retrofit, usually cost much more per square foot to air-condition and heat. Builders have to follow very strict guidelines in new homes and additions, observing the latest earthquake safety standards—a critical factor for developments in North Bengal.

In general, new homes are usually more fire-safe and better accommodating of new security and garage-door systems. However, buyers should be cautious: new homes that possess a smooth veneer might reveal the use of substandard building materials or shoddy workmanship over time if not built by a reputed developer.
